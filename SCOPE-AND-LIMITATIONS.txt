SmartBiz Hub: Online Customer, Order, and Billing Platform

PROFESSOR'S SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
Transcribed from the scope image supplied by the student. This replaces the
previous proposed expansion; the professor's scope is the governing reference.

Scope
The system will handle customer registration, order taking (walk-in or online
order entry), billing/invoicing, order status tracking, and basic customer
communication (order confirmation). It aims to streamline the order-to-billing
process and improve customer service for businesses that take orders.

Limitations
- Does not include full inventory deduction automation linked to complex production.
- No advanced CRM features (marketing campaigns, loyalty points, or customer
  segmentation analytics).
- Payment collection is recorded manually or via proof of payment (no live
  payment gateway).
- Limited to single-business use (no multi-tenant SaaS features).
- No delivery route optimization or real-time tracking.

Possible Modules (Examples)
- Customer Management Module
- Order Entry & Management
- Billing / Invoice Generation
- Order Status Tracking
- Basic Customer Notifications
- Sales & Order Reports

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES — NOT CHANGES TO THE PROFESSOR'S SCOPE
The existing prototype uses manual payment recording rather than proof uploads.
Its basic customer communication is an on-screen order confirmation and order
status page. It does not send email/SMS or use courier location tracking.

REQUIRED ERD AND IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY
The student confirmed that the professor also requires the 11-entity ERD.
Keep User, Supplier, Purchase Order, Purchase Order Item, Product, Stock
Management, Customer, Customer Order, Order Item, Billing, and Payment.

Purchasing and inventory are supporting functions for those required entities:
supplier records, purchase orders, partial/full receiving, stock balances,
reservation for customer orders, deduction on completion, and release on
cancellation. There is no manufacturing workflow, bill of materials, production
scheduling, or deduction based on complex production. This implementation note
does not replace or expand the professor's scope quoted above.

SCOPE TO CODE MAP
Customer management: StoreController registration/profile and ManagementController
customer records, using User and Customer. Registration creates both records
in one transaction; only the customer's own orders can be viewed by that login.
Order entry: StoreController checkout and TransactionController walk-in entry,
using CustomerOrder and OrderItem. ShopService validates quantities and stock.
Billing/invoicing: ShopService::bill and billing views, using Billing. A bill is
required before staff can record payments.
Manual payments: ShopService::pay and billing/show.blade.php, using Payment.
Amounts reduce the balance; a method and reference never trigger a transfer.
Order status and basic communication: components/order-update.blade.php and
orders/show.blade.php use the saved CustomerOrder status. Confirmation includes
an order reference, current step, and what happens next. No new notification
table, email/SMS delivery, or read/unread notification inbox is introduced.
Reports: ManagementController reports and manage/reports.blade.php summarize
orders, sales and manually recorded payments, with supporting purchase/stock
summaries from the required ERD entities.
Delivery: staff manually enters the courier reference and advances order status.
There is no live courier integration, real-time location or route optimization.

DEFENSE DEMONSTRATION
1. Register a customer; show the corresponding Customers entry as Admin/Staff.
2. Place an online order and show its order number and on-screen confirmation.
3. Show staff walk-in order entry as the alternative ordering method.
4. Process the order and generate its bill before recording payment.
5. Record a partial payment and then the remaining amount; show the updated
   balance, printable invoice and receipts. Explain that no money is transferred.
6. For pickup, mark ready and complete after full payment. For delivery, mark
   ready, enter a courier reference, dispatch after full payment, then complete.
7. Reopen the customer's order page to show the current step and next-step text.
8. Show sales/order reports. Demonstrate purchase receiving and basic product
   stock movements as the supporting workflow for the required ERD.

This is a single-business school prototype for One Optics Clinic. It includes
no advanced CRM, multi-tenant SaaS, proof-of-payment upload, clinical records,
automated refunds, or manufacturing functions. Payment-record voiding corrects
a record only; it does not return money through a bank or gateway.
