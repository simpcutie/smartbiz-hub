SMARTBIZ HUB — SCOPE AUDIT
Review date: 2026-10-02
Basis: professor's scope image and student's confirmation that all 11 ERD
entities are required. This is a code/test assessment, not an adviser sign-off.

RESULT
No conflict found with the stated functional scope and five limitations when
read together with the required 11-entity ERD. No feature removal was needed.
Purchasing and basic stock handling are supporting ERD functions, not complex
production automation. They are not explicitly named in the short scope alone.

REQUIRED FUNCTIONS — VERIFIED
1. Customer registration: StoreController::signup creates a User and Customer
   together. The customer appears in Admin/Staff customer records.
2. Online orders: session cart, selected-item checkout, CustomerOrder/OrderItem
   persistence and ownership checks. Existing HTTP workflow tests pass.
3. Walk-in orders: staff selects a customer and items through TransactionController
   and ShopService. New ScopeWorkflowTest verifies HTTP entry through billing.
4. Billing/invoicing: bill generation precedes staff payment entry; invoices and
   receipts render from the saved records. Partial/full balances are calculated.
5. Order status: staff-controlled status transitions and customer-visible current
   stage/next-step messages. Cancelled orders show no active progress track.
6. Basic customer communication: on-screen order confirmation/reference and
   account order details. No email/SMS or notification inbox is claimed.
7. Sales/order reports: date-filtered summaries and CSV exports. Supporting
   purchase/stock summaries are based on the same required ERD entities.

LIMITATIONS — VERIFIED
1. No full inventory deduction automation linked to complex production:
   receiving adds product stock; orders reserve it; cancellation releases it;
   completion deducts it once. No production jobs, materials consumption,
   manufacturing recipes, bills of materials or production scheduling exist.
   Basic order-linked deduction DOES exist and is required by the ERD flow;
   do not describe the system as having no inventory automation whatsoever.
2. No advanced CRM: no campaigns, loyalty points or segmentation features were
   found in application routes, models, controllers or schema. Basic customer
   records and order reports are present.
3. Manual payment OR proof: the manual-recording option is implemented. Staff
   enters the amount, method and reference. GCash/Maya/Card/Bank Transfer are
   method labels, not integrations. No payment gateway or proof upload exists.
   Voiding corrects a record and never initiates a refund.
4. Single-business use: one clinic configuration, no tenant model, tenant routing
   or multi-business selector. Multiple customer accounts are not multi-tenancy.
5. No route optimization or real-time tracking: delivery uses staff-entered
   statuses and courier reference text. No GPS, courier API or live polling
   exists in the application code. Clinic Maps link is a static location link.

DATABASE AND ACCESS
ErdSchemaTest verifies exactly 11 business tables and models. Laravel migration
bookkeeping is excluded from the business count. Role middleware and ownership
checks protect management functions, invoices, receipts and customer orders.
No new business entities or migrations were introduced during this audit.

VERIFICATION
24 tests passed, 281 assertions. Tests use isolated database fixtures.
New ScopeWorkflowTest verifies registration -> dashboard customer listing ->
walk-in order -> processing -> bill -> manually recorded GCash payment ->
customer order/invoice/receipt -> Admin reports. It also verifies customers
cannot access the staff payment endpoint and asserts no HTTP requests, mail,
queued mail or notifications are sent during this tested flow.
Existing tests cover online checkout, stock rollback, reservation/completion,
partial payments, duplicate submissions, roles, ownership, report exports,
order progress and the 11-entity schema.
Static review covered routes, business services, schema, dependencies, custom
browser scripts, controllers and relevant views. Tests do not prove every
possible runtime state or guarantee academic acceptance.

DEFENSE WORDING
Payment is recorded manually; balances are calculated automatically.
Order confirmation and updates are displayed inside the website.
Delivery status is updated by staff, not tracked live.
Inventory is basic product stock management, not production management.
